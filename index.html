What KIT means as a brand

More than a name. A scalable business platform.

KIT is designed to give a buyer an immediate route into modern home-improvement retail. It can launch as a standalone ecommerce business, extend the reach of an established bricks-and-mortar operator, or introduce a distinct new brand aimed at a different customer segment.

The shared master identity creates recognition across every category, while the specialist names make each offer clear and commercially focused. A buyer can begin with one category and expand over time without rebuilding the brand from the ground up.

That makes KIT suitable for diversification, new-market entry, digital expansion and the creation of an additional revenue channel alongside an existing business.

Designed to support
  • Ecommerce-first retailers
  • Established kitchen and interiors companies
  • Builders’ merchants and trade suppliers
  • Manufacturers seeking a direct-to-consumer route
  • Franchise and multi-location operators
  • National and international expansion
